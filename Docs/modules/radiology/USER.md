# Radiology — User Guide

This guide details how to track radiology requests, register clinical findings, upload reports, and approve scans.

---

## 1. Opening the Radiology Queue
1. Open the **Radiology Worklist** portal (`/radiology`).
2. The page loads three queue tabs:
   * **Pending Scans**: Incoming radiology requests waiting for imaging.
   * **Uploaded / Review**: Completed scans with findings logged, awaiting radiologist sign-off.
   * **Approved Reports**: Finalized report sheets ready to be printed and archived.

---

## 2. Uploading Scan Findings
1. In the **Pending Scans** tab, locate the patient and click **Upload Report**.
2. Type structural observations into the **Clinical Findings** field.
3. Log final medical diagnoses in the **Diagnostic Impression** field.
4. (Optional) Paste the PACS image reference URL or DICOM file link in the attachment field.
5. Click **Submit Report for Verification**. The scan advances to the *Uploaded / Review* queue.

---

## 3. Radiologist Sign-Off & Print
1. In the **Uploaded / Review** tab, click **Review & Sign-Off**.
2. Review findings, clinical indications, and impression summary.
3. Click **Sign-Off & Approve Report** to finalize the sheet.
4. Once finalized, click **Print Report** (uses standard browser print layouts on hospital letterhead) for physical distribution.
