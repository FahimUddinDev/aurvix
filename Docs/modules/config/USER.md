# Config & Localization — User Guide

## Getting Started

To configure system defaults and hospital properties, you must be logged in as an **Administrator**
or a **Super Admin**.

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Managing Hospital Profiles

1. Log in and go to **Settings** &rarr; **Hospital Settings** in the sidebar.
2. Update the hospital's English and Bengali names and addresses.
3. Verify that the contact email, website, and phone number (`+880XXXXXXXXXX`) are correct.
4. Select the default timezone (e.g. `Asia/Dhaka`) and currency.
5. Define the patient identification prefix (e.g., `AVX`). This prefix will be prepended to all
   newly registered patients.
6. Click **Save Settings** to apply.

### Configuring Branches

1. Go to **Settings** &rarr; **Branch Management** in the sidebar.
2. A list of all hospital branches will be shown.
3. To add a new location, click **Create Branch**.
4. Enter the branch's name (English/Bengali), address, and contact number. Click **Save**.
5. To change a branch's address or temporarily suspend it, click **Edit** next to the branch, toggle
   the **Active** checkbox, and click **Save**.

### Managing Departments

1. Go to **Settings** &rarr; **Department Management** in the sidebar.
2. To create a new department, click **Create Department**.
3. Fill in the Department Name, Bengali name, and a unique **uppercase department code** (e.g.,
   `CARD` for Cardiology).
4. Provide a description of the department and click **Save**.
5. To disable a department, click **Edit**, uncheck **Active**, and click **Save**.

### Editing System Config Defaults

1. Go to **Settings** &rarr; **System Defaults** in the sidebar.
2. Select a category tab (e.g. PATIENT, BILLING) to filter parameters.
3. Locate the setting you wish to modify and click **Change**.
4. Input the new value:
   - For **Boolean** settings, select "Enabled" or "Disabled" from the dropdown.
   - For **Number** settings, enter a valid numeric value.
5. Click **Save** to apply the configuration.

### Switching Languages (EN / বাংলা)

1. At the top-right corner of the topbar header, click the **English / বাংলা** toggle button.
2. The entire application interface will immediately adapt to your chosen language.
3. Your choice is saved to your user profile so you do not have to switch it again when logging in
   next time.
