# Doctor & Department Directory — User Guide

This guide describes how to use the Doctor Directory, manage schedules, and assign clinical fees in the Aurevix HMS.

---

## 1. Navigating the Doctor Directory
1. Click on **Doctors** in the left sidebar menu (under the *Clinical* section).
2. Use the **search bar** at the top of the directory to filter medical professionals by name, BMDC registration number, qualifications, or specialization.
3. Use the **All Departments** dropdown to filter search results to a specific clinical department (e.g., Cardiology, General Medicine, Pediatrics).

---

## 2. Viewing a Doctor Profile
1. From the main directory, click the **Profile** button in the actions column for the respective doctor.
2. In the profile view, you will find:
   * Professional credentials,BMDC number, and specialization.
   * Full biographical profiles in English and Bengali.
   * Contact details (Email and Phone).
   * Summary of configured weekly schedules and clinic timings.

---

## 3. Registering a New Doctor Profile
> **Note**: An admin must first create a user account for the medical staff member in **Settings > User Management** (with the *Doctor* role).

1. In the **Doctors Directory** screen, click the **+ Add Doctor Profile** button in the top right.
2. Select the doctor's user account from the **Select User** dropdown.
3. Assign them to their primary clinical department.
4. Input their BMDC registration code and primary consultation fee in BDT.
5. Provide qualifications (e.g. MBBS, FCPS), experience years, and biographical text.
6. Click **Register** to submit.

---

## 4. Configuring Weekly Schedule Slots
1. Navigate to the doctor's profile page and click **Manage** beside *Weekly Schedule* (or click **Schedule** directly from the main directory listing).
2. On the schedule editor screen:
   * **Left Panel**: Configure a new timing slot. Select the day of the week (Sunday through Saturday), clinic start time (e.g., 09:00), end time (e.g., 13:00), slot duration (typically 15 minutes), and the maximum patient capacity for that window.
   * Click **+ Add to List** to stage the slot. Staged slots are checked for overlap conflicts on the same day.
   * **Right Panel**: Lists all pending slots. Review the slots or click **Remove** to delete.
3. Click **Save Schedule** in the bottom right to apply the weekly clinic hours.
